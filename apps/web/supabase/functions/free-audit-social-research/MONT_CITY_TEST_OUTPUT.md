# Mont City Expected Output (Reference)

This is the expected strategist output shape for the Mont City Media Group / Mont City Network test case after this upgrade.

## Expected Detections

- Parent brand: `Mont City Media Group`
- Public/media platform: `Mont City Network`
- Brand architecture note includes parent vs platform distinction
- Conversion path does **not** default to `Book a Strategy Session`

## Expected CTA Direction

- Primary CTA likely one of:
  - `Request Event Coverage`
  - `Book a Production Consultation`
- Secondary CTAs should include options such as:
  - `Submit a Story`
  - `Sponsor a Series`
  - `Partner With Mont City`
  - `Get Featured`
  - `Request a Live Stream Quote`

## Expected Business + Audience Interpretation

- Business model should identify media + production revenue streams
- Audience should separate:
  - buyer
  - viewer
  - subject
  - sponsor
  - partner
  - community
- Findings should include business implication, revenue implication, and audience implication

## Expected Cultural + Proof Direction

- Cultural context should emphasize local identity and community storytelling value
- Proof inventory should prioritize reels, recap samples, livestream examples, featured stories, documentary/project proof, testimonials, and partner logos
