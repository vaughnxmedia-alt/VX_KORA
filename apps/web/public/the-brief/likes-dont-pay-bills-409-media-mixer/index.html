Likes Don’t Pay Bills: A Real Conversation From the 409 Media Mixer

Jocelyn “Jah Jah” Vaughn of JahKnowsPR Firm shares key insights from the 3rd Annual 409 Media Mixer, discussing vanity metrics, authentic branding, audience connection, and the future of Southeast Texas media.

J JahKnowsPR FirmBrand Strategist
May 28, 2026 2-4 Min

The 3rd Annual 409 Media Mixer was more than a networking event. It reflected a growing shift happening within Southeast Texas media and creative culture.

As the founder of JahKnowsPR Firm, Jocelyn “Jah Jah” Vaughn was invited to serve as a panelist alongside Piarre Armstrong and Lauren Bebeau. Together, the discussion explored branding, visibility, audience connection, and the realities of building influence in today’s digital landscape.

One of the biggest conversations throughout the evening centered around the reality of social media success.

While many creators today are heavily focused on going viral, gaining followers, and chasing likes, the panelists discussed how vanity metrics can often create a false sense of success. A large following may look impressive online, but followers do not automatically create opportunities, revenue, partnerships, or long-term business growth.

There are people with huge platforms who still struggle to pay their bills because attention alone is not enough.

The conversation shifted toward the importance of strategy, positioning, trust, and genuine audience connection. Audiences are becoming more intentional about who they support. People want authenticity, clear messaging, and brands that truly understand the people they serve.

Another major takeaway was the importance of collaboration. Southeast Texas is filled with talented creatives, entrepreneurs, media personalities, and innovators. Events like the 409 Media Mixer create spaces where those individuals can connect, build relationships, and strengthen the region’s creative ecosystem together.

The future of Southeast Texas media will not belong only to the loudest voices online. It will belong to the people who know how to build meaningful relationships, communicate with purpose, and turn visibility into real value.

The culture is evolving.

And conversations like these are helping push it forward.
